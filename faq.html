Frequently asked questions

A few things to know before you begin.

Genealogy research can raise as many questions as it answers. Here are some practical notes about working together.

What information should I prepare?

Share what you are comfortable providing: the research question, names of deceased relatives, approximate dates and places, and any sources you have already checked. Mark uncertain details as estimates. Please do not send identity documents or sensitive information about living people in your first message.

How much does genealogy research cost?

Fees depend on the agreed scope, record access, geography, complexity and deliverables. We discuss a written proposal and fee before research starts. The package descriptions are indicative starting points, not fixed prices.

How long will my project take?

Timeframes depend on the research question, availability of sources and whether archives or certificates need to be accessed. We provide an estimate with the proposed scope and let you know if circumstances change.

Can you guarantee that you will find my ancestor?

No. Records may not survive, may be inaccessible or may not identify a person conclusively. We can commit to the agreed research work, but not to a particular discovery. Findings and limitations are reported honestly.

Do you research outside Australia?

Research may extend to other countries where the question and accessible sources support it. We discuss geographic scope and practical access before preparing a proposal.

What is a GEDCOM file?

GEDCOM is a widely used text format for exchanging genealogical data between family-tree programs. It can transfer names, dates, places and relationships, but formatting or source notes may not transfer identically between software.

Can you include my photographs and family documents?

Possibly, depending on the project. We discuss image quality, attribution, copyright, permissions and how materials will be used before including them in a deliverable. Please do not upload sensitive files through an unconfigured contact form.

How do you protect living relatives’ privacy?

We minimise personal information, keep living people’s details out of public examples and agree how any relevant sensitive material is handled. See the privacy policy for information about this website and contact the business before sharing sensitive research materials.

What if my family research includes a sensitive discovery?

We approach sensitive findings with care and will discuss how they are presented within the agreed scope. We do not assume every discovery should be shared with every family member or published in a keepsake.

Can you continue research I have already started?

Yes. We can discuss reviewing existing trees or notes, checking sources and identifying next steps. An initial enquiry need only describe the research and does not require you to send private documents.

Still wondering?

Ask us about your particular question.

We are happy to help you understand what might be possible before you decide on a research project.

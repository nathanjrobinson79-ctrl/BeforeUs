Built with sources

Each connection needs a reason.

A family tree is only as useful as the evidence behind it. Names can change, ages vary and records may describe people with the same name. Research notes help explain the basis for relationships and flag uncertainty.

  • Documented sources attached to research conclusions
  • Relationships represented clearly across generations
  • Spelling variants, conflicting dates and gaps noted honestly
  • GEDCOM export available where included in the agreed scope
  • Privacy-conscious treatment of living relatives

The people behind the research

Rooted in curiosity. Guided by evidence.

Before Us is a genealogy and family-history consultancy devoted to finding the people behind the records, and telling their stories with care.

Our purpose

To understand the past, without losing sight of people.

A family tree is more than a chart. It is an invitation to ask better questions about migration, work, community and the circumstances that shaped a life.

We approach each project with respect for the family’s knowledge and the limits of the historical record. Findings are supported by sources; unanswered questions are named rather than glossed over.

A record is a clue, not the whole story.

Our principles

Care at every step.

I

Evidence first

We look for original or reliable records, cite sources and consider whether evidence supports a conclusion.

II

Context matters

Dates and places gain meaning when set alongside the historical conditions people lived through.

III

Privacy by design

We minimise collection of personal information and handle living people’s details discreetly.

A transparent process

Research you can follow.

We agree the research question and deliverables before work begins. If records conflict, are unavailable or point in a different direction, we explain that plainly. Research can reveal new questions as well as answers.

Respect for complex histories

Family histories may include adoption, separation, displacement, conflict or other sensitive experiences. We take a considered approach, discuss what you wish to explore and do not assume that every discovery should be shared widely.

Start where you are

Curiosity is enough to begin.

Share the question you have been carrying. We can help identify a sensible next step, whether or not you have a complete family tree.

Privacy policy

Private family information deserves care.

This notice explains the privacy-conscious design of this website. The business owner must review and update it before launch to match actual operations.

Website privacy

What this site collects

This static website does not use advertising trackers or analytics by default. The contact form prepares an email in your email app; it does not send information to the website. Information is sent only if you review and send the message, and is then handled by your email provider and the business’s mailbox. A hosting provider may process technical request data (such as IP address, browser details and requested page) to deliver and secure the site; check the provider’s privacy information for its practices.

Enquiries and third-party services

If you send an enquiry by email, the details you include are processed by your email provider and the business to respond. The business should explain its purpose, storage, retention period and privacy contact here. Do not include sensitive personal information about living relatives in a general enquiry.

Genealogy research and family information

Research projects may involve information about family members. The scope, permissions, access, storage, sharing and retention of research materials should be agreed directly with the business before any sensitive material is transferred. Information about living people should be minimised and handled discreetly. This public website does not publish private client trees or customer testimonials.

External resources

The website loads web fonts from Google Fonts and a background photograph from Unsplash. Those providers may receive technical connection data when the resources load. The site owner can replace these external resources with locally hosted, appropriately licensed alternatives if preferred.

Your choices and questions

Do not send sensitive personal information through a general enquiry form. For privacy questions or requests about your information, email beforeusancestry@gmail.com. Any applicable privacy rights or complaint pathways depend on the business's circumstances.

Owner action before launch

Review this notice against the business’s actual practices, hosting, form provider, recordkeeping and applicable Australian privacy obligations. Add the responsible entity, effective date and retention arrangements. This page is general website copy, not legal advice.

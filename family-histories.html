Family histories

Give the names on the tree a voice.

A family history brings people, places and the records that survive into a story your family can read, question and keep.

Research, shaped into narrative

A story grounded in what we can know.

Civil registrations, newspapers, letters, photographs and local histories can reveal different parts of a life. We bring those sources together, with historical context and clear references.

We distinguish documented facts from interpretation and acknowledge the silences in the record. A vivid story need not invent details to feel human.

A record, a place, a life.

Possible formats

A family keepsake, in a form that suits you.

The final format depends on research scope, materials, permissions and your preferences.

I

Ancestral biography

A focused account of one person or couple, shaped around the records and historical context available.

II

Branch history

A broader look at a family line, tracing relationships, migration, work and community through generations.

III

Heirloom book

A carefully planned research book with an agreed scope, editorial approach and discussion of image rights and print production.

Photographs and family memories

Make space for what your family already knows.

Family photographs, letters and oral histories can add valuable perspective. We discuss how materials will be used, identify what can be dated or verified, and respect copyright and family permissions.

Thoughtful about difficult stories

Not every discovery belongs in every edition. We agree how sensitive content should be handled, particularly where living relatives or private family matters are involved.

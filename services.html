How it works

Clear expectations, careful findings.

  1. Tell us what you know

    We discuss your question, relevant family knowledge, privacy needs and preferred outcome.

  2. Agree a scope

    We propose suitable sources and deliverables, along with a written quote and timeframe before starting.

  3. Receive your research

    We share findings with citations and explain limitations, uncertainty and useful next steps.

Research with integrity

Not every clue leads to a clear answer.

Records can be incomplete, inaccessible or contradictory. We do not promise that every line can be traced, and we will not fill gaps with unsupported assumptions. The research plan and fee are discussed with you before any work begins.

We respect living people

For an initial enquiry, please avoid sending identity documents or sensitive details about living relatives. We can discuss an appropriate way to share information if it is relevant to the agreed research.
